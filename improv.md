# Human–Computer Improv

A 30-minute follow-up to the [AI Case Challenge](./), played on phones with the [Oslo Mobile Orchestra](https://fourms.github.io/omo/) apps in the browser, no installation needed. First run at Sandbjerg, 23 November 2026, straight after the case.

The idea follows from the case: four rounds in which the machine listens, learns, proposes, and finally steps back. After each round, ask who is making the music.

**Play now:** [fourms.github.io/omo](https://fourms.github.io/omo/)

## Before you start

- Check that the room's Wi-Fi or mobile data reaches the site, and open it once on the leader's phone so it is cached.
- Phones: iPhone in Safari, Android in Chrome, not inside a mail or chat app. Silent mode off, volume up; allow microphone and motion when asked.
- Each app has its own QR button for sharing.
- Seat the room in three sections (left, middle, right) for round 3.

## The rounds


| Time | Round | App | What happens | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17.30 | Set-up, 3 min | Hub | Everyone opens OMO and turns Audio on | |
| 17.33 | 1. The machine listens, 6 min | [Firefly](https://fourms.github.io/omo/apps/firefly/) | Every phone pulses and listens for the others' clicks, nudging its own pulse; start at different tempos, put the phones down and listen until they lock. Then try to resist with your own taps | Who is keeping time? |
| 17.39 | 2. The machine learns, 7 min | [Train & Shake](https://fourms.github.io/omo/apps/train-shake/) | Record three examples each of still, sway and shake; then play the model with gestures, and Alexander conducts the three gestures | What did your model learn, and what confuses it (your phone, your body, your examples)? |
| 17.46 | 3. The machine proposes, 7 min | [Just Equal](https://fourms.github.io/omo/apps/just-equal/) (left), [Markov Melody](https://fourms.github.io/omo/apps/markov-melody/) (middle), [Evo Drumkit](https://fourms.github.io/omo/apps/evo-drumkit/) (right) | A drone, a melody generated from a Markov chain, and drums that evolve as players choose; the humans curate what the machine offers | Who is the composer when you only choose? |
| 17.53 | 4. The machine steps back, 4 min | [Standstill](https://fourms.github.io/omo/apps/standstill/) | Everyone stands, phone to the chest; sound blooms only when you are still, and any movement silences it. One short heat, eyes closed if you like | Now who is performing? |
| 17.57 | Close, 3 min | | Back to the case: the drum model, the performer on the recording, and what should be credited | |

## If something fails

- No sound: Audio on (top right), raise the phone volume, silent mode off; on iPhone, tap the screen once.
- Microphone or motion refused: reload the app page and allow; in round 2 those who cannot use motion join someone who can.
- Network trouble: play in pairs on the phones that loaded; Standstill and Just Equal run without the network once loaded.
- Too loud: the Volume bar under the header, 50 to 150%.
